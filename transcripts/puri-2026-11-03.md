## 4. Healthcare-relevant items (record as Minority Leader)

**Verbatim, Apr. 2026, Marquette.** Committee to Protect Health Care "Saving Rural Health Care Town Hall" (Radio Results Network, 4/9/2026):
> "There are actions that the Michigan Legislature could be taking right now to improve health care access"
> "but Republicans are using their majority in the House to block them"
> "From creating a Prescription Drug Affordability Board to rein in drug prices"
> "to creating more oversight for health care mergers"
> "to defending against federal Medicaid cuts"
> "we have solutions to this crisis"
> "We just need more leaders in the Legislature who will put patients over politics."

(These are fragments in the order returned; they appear to come from one passage.) **MHA relevance:** a Prescription Drug Affordability Board, and in particular **increased oversight of health care mergers/consolidation**, which bears directly on hospital M&A.

**FY27 budget fight, Apr. 2026** (Michigan Public, 4/23/2026):
> "We have a speaker that claims to have a close relationship with the president."
- *[Paraphrase]* Puri blamed House GOP leadership for siding with President Trump and **H.R. 1**, the federal law he said cut funding. The House GOP budget assumed **$300M less in Medicaid spending** as federal work requirements take effect.

**$645M clawback, Dec. 2025–Jan. 2026** (housedems.com headlines/summaries):
- 12/17/2025: "House Democrats fight to restore $645 million cut for kids with cancer, new moms and infants." The release says House Republicans rejected restoring the money more than a dozen times.
- 1/7/2026: Statement on AG Nessel's opinion that the GOP clawback of $645M was unconstitutional. Speaker Hall vowed to sue (gophouse.org, 1/7/2026). The current status of that dispute was not found.

**H.R. 1 / Medicaid, July 30, 2025:** House Democrats' "As Medicaid Turns 60" release warned that H.R. 1 ("One Big Beautiful Bill Act") cuts put care for hundreds of thousands of Michiganders at risk. Not confirmed as a Puri quote.

**Not found:** any Puri statement on **nurse staffing ratios** (e.g., Safe Patient Care Act), hospital provider taxes/directed payments, certificate of need, or scope of practice. His stated health priorities are drug affordability, merger oversight, defending Medicaid, and rural access.
