# Winnie Brinks: Election Night and Post-Election Remarks

- **Speaker:** Winnie Brinks (D-Grand Rapids), Michigan Senate Majority Leader (the first woman in that role), Lieutenant Governor-elect
- **Office won:** Lieutenant Governor of Michigan, as Jocelyn Benson's running mate. With Benson, she is part of the state's first all-female governor and lieutenant governor team. As lieutenant governor she will preside over the Senate and break ties.
- **Event:** Michigan Democratic Party watch party, Hudson's Detroit. She spoke at about 9:15 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 3, before the race was called (WOOD/Yahoo). She then issued a written statement on the Senate results around Nov. 4.

## Post-election statements (as outgoing Senate Majority Leader)

From her statement on the Senate results (Michigan Advance/Yahoo, Nov. 4):
- "Since winning back the majority for the first time in nearly 40 years in 2022, we knew we would have a new mission in 2026"
- "This newly expanded majority will allow Senate Democrats to reach new heights."
- "I look forward to working with our new caucus as Lieutenant Governor to drive costs down and wages up"
- PARAPHRASE: The Senate's top priority will be a Michigan Voting Rights Act. (Michigan Advance)
- **Successor as Majority Leader:** Not yet named. Senate Democrats must choose a new leader before the 2027 session. (Michigan Advance)
