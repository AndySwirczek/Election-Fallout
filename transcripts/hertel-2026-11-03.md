## 1. Verbatim quotes

### Written statement on the trifecta, via Michigan Advance (fetched directly)
> "when Democrats are in power, we make progress for working people."

> "With our last trifecta, we restored workers' rights, cut taxes for families, fed kids at school"

*(A search summary gives the fuller sentence: "...fed kids at school, and lifted tens of thousands of children out of poverty." That continuation is snippet-only.)*

### Watch-party speech, Detroit *(snippets, unverified)*
- Opened by quoting U.S. Rep. Haley Stevens's campaign line and vowing to "stick it to 'em." The headline says Hertel quoted Stevens. Note that Stevens lost the U.S. Senate primary to Abdul El-Sayed and later endorsed Republican Mike Rogers.
> "We're going to make Mike Rogers a two-time loser."
- Asked whether Democratic wins were bigger than expected: "Of course."
- On El-Sayed: "He doesn't have all of these corporate donors"

### Congressional statements *(snippets, unverified)*
- On Rep. Hillary Scholten's re-election (MI-03): her commitment to fighting for Michiganders is "fierce and unwavering."
- On Rep. Debbie Dingell (MI-06): "the definition of a fighter."

## 2. Paraphrase (marked)
- *[Paraphrase, snippet]* The written statement pledged to keep building a Michigan where people can thrive under Governor-elect Jocelyn Benson's leadership.
- *[Paraphrase, Michigan Public 2025]* When elected chair in Feb. 2025, Hertel called restoring the Democratic trifecta a top priority. The party has now achieved that.
