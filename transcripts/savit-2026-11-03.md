### Takeaways for MHA (inference, not sourced statements)
- **Hospital M&A is his most specific health pledge.** Expect closer AG antitrust and charitable-trust review of hospital affiliations, especially rural and U.P. deals, with continuity of services as an explicit test.
- **Consumer Protection Act rulemaking** in the first 100 days is aimed at AI and price gouging. Watch whether the rules reach health care billing, pricing algorithms, or debt collection.
- **The federal-litigation posture continues from Nessel.** Expect suits against federal Medicaid/H.R.1 implementation actions he views as unlawful. This is likely aligned with hospital interests on funding.
- **Reproductive health:** strongly protective. Little practical friction with hospitals is expected.
