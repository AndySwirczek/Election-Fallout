## Specific healthcare priorities by member

### Debbie Dingell (MI-6)
- **Signature priority: home and community-based services (HCBS) and home care.**
  - She co-led the **HCBS Access Act (H.R. 8540)** with Rep. Schakowsky, introduced Apr. 30, 2026. Per search summaries of govinfo and Schakowsky's release, it would make HCBS a permanent Medicaid benefit, raise funding for HCBS and the direct-care workforce, and phase out HCBS waiting lists.
  - She also introduced the **Long-Term Care Workforce Support Act (H.R. 8541)**, on direct-care pay and conditions.
  - She previously led the **Better Care Better Jobs Act** (118th Congress).
  - Sources: https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BILLS-119hr8540ih and https://schakowsky.house.gov/media/press-releases/schakowsky-dingell-introduce-legislation-expand-home-care-strengthen-care
- **Medicaid work requirements.** In July 2026 she urged withdrawal of the proposed CMS rule "Medicaid Program Community Engagement Requirement for Certain Individuals," which tightens exemptions from the OBBBA work requirement. She said: "Medicaid is the main funder of home and community-based services." (WEMU, Jul. 29, 2026: https://www.wemu.org/wemu-news/2026-07-29/rep-dingell-urges-public-to-express-dissatisfaction-with-proposed-medicaid-changes)
- **Victory remarks:** none with healthcare content were found.

### Hillary Scholten (MI-3)
- **ACA enhanced subsidies, made permanent.** At the May 2025 Rules Committee session on H.R. 1, she offered an amendment to make the enhanced ACA premium tax credits permanent. It would remove the 400% FPL cap and hold premiums to at most 8.5% of income.
  - "Republicans are trying to take health care away from people while they sleep and they are hoping no one notices,"
  - Source: https://scholten.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-scholten-fights-protect-health-care-millions-during-all-night-rules (May 21, 2025)
- **Victory remarks:** none with healthcare content were found. No 2026-specific health bill was found.

### Kristen McDonald Rivet (MI-8)
- **Named bills** (press release, May 20, 2025: https://mcdonaldrivet.house.gov/media/press-releases/mcdonald-rivet-announces-new-efforts-expand-health-care-access-mid-michigan). The release does not make clear whether she sponsors or cosponsors each one:
  - **Prescription Drug Transparency and Affordability Act**
  - **Mamas and Babies in Underserved Communities Act**
  - **SAFE Act** (fall-risk assessments in the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit)
  - **Medicare Patient Access and Practice Stabilization Act** (physician payment)
  - FY2026 earmark requests: a CMU mobile medical simulation vehicle with a rural focus, and Ten16 Recovery Network SUD facilities.
- **Medicaid cuts and hospitals.** She opposed the H.R. 1 Medicaid cuts. Per search summaries of WCMU and WNEM from 2025, she said the cuts would defund mid-Michigan hospitals and threaten rural hospitals, but her exact wording was not verified.
- **Flint.** She pursued water-crisis health funding in the FY2026 HHS budget (search summary).
- **Victory remarks:** none with healthcare content were found.

### Rashida Tlaib (MI-12)
- **Medical debt.** She introduced the **Consumer Protection for Medical Debt Collections Act**, which would bar medical-debt collection for two years and keep "medically necessary" debt off credit reports (https://tlaib.house.gov/posts/tlaib-re-introduces-bill-to-remove-medically-necessary-debt-from-credit-reports).
  - A tracker lists **H.R. 4827, the Medical Debt Relief Act of 2025**, under her name.
  - She co-leads the **Medical Debt Cancellation Act** with Sanders, Khanna and Merkley (2024).
- **Coverage gap.** The Michigan Public live blog names her priority as a ban on federal agencies using automated mass surveillance, which is not health-related. No healthcare victory remarks were found.
- **Medicare for All.** She has a long record of support. Her 2026-specific statements were not checked.

### Donavan McKinney (MI-13)
- **Medicare for All.**
  - "We need Medicare for All. At the end of the day, people can't afford life's necessities, including healthcare." (WDET, Apr. 1, 2026: https://wdet.org/2026/04/01/state-rep-donavan-mckinney-talks-issues-13th-congressional-primary/)
  - Bridge lists his top priorities as universal child care, Medicare for All, and getting corporate money out of politics.
- **ACA.** On the ACA: "it's a damn shame that Republicans are attacking the ACA" (WDET).
- **Medicaid timing.** Per AFP on Nov. 3, he accused Trump of postponing major healthcare cuts until after the election.
- **Victory remarks:** none were found.
