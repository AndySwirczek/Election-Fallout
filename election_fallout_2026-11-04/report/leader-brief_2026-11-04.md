# Election Fallout: Leader Brief

**What Michigan's new majority said about health care, Nov. 3–4, 2026.** This is the short version of the 20-page report (`election_health_2026-11-04.pdf`). It covers only remarks made election night and the day after.

## Bottom line

1. **Only 3 of 6 leaders on camera talked about health care:** El-Sayed (21 of 28 statements), Dingell (5) and Benson (2). Brinks, Hertel and McMorrow did not.
2. **The clearest hospital signal is reversing the federal Medicaid cuts.** Dingell called it "one of the first things" the new U.S. House majority will do. El-Sayed named it his top Senate priority and warned that coverage losses push patients into emergency rooms.
3. **State leaders stayed general.** Benson tied health care to affordability and women's access. Likely Speaker Puri said he "watched healthcare be ripped away from our sick" (Michigan Advance). No one named a state health bill.

## What MHA should watch

| Signal | Who said it | Why it matters for hospitals |
