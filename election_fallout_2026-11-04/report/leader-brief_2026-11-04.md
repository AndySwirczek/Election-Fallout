# Election Fallout: Leader Brief

**What Michigan's new majority said after the election, Nov. 3–4, 2026.** Part 1 covers the main themes across lawmakers. Part 2 focuses on health care. Quotes are verbatim. The PDF version is `election_health_2026-11-04_brief.pdf`; the full 20-page report is `election_health_2026-11-04.pdf`.

## Part 1: Key themes across lawmakers

### Lowering everyday costs
*Benson · Brinks · Dingell · McMorrow*

The most common theme. Leaders named groceries, rent, gas, housing, child care, energy and health care, and tied the election result to voters' frustration with prices.

- **Benson:** "lower our costs and raise our wages and end corruption" ([transition press conference, Detroit 4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xha9JCqPbBQ&t=270s))
- **Brinks:** "together we will deliver on our commitment to make life more affordable for all" ([transition press conference, Detroit 5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xha9JCqPbBQ&t=330s))
- **Dingell:** "the number one thing on everybody's mind working families is how much groceries were costing" ([post-election interview, FOX 2 Detroit 1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBwr5WNZbwg&t=77s))

### Corruption, corporate power and money in politics
*Benson · El-Sayed · Savit*

Benson and Savit framed their wins as siding with people over "the powerful." El-Sayed called for getting money out of politics and taking on large corporations that raise prices.

- **Benson:** "fight the corruption that too often protects the … powerful and hurts the people." ([victory speech, Hudson's Detroit 4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X0E_y4UhEaM&t=294s))
- **El-Sayed:** "I don't think that you should be left to a few big corporations to just consistently raise rates on you" ([post-election interview, WDIV Local 4 3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WL7ZL2UENhI&t=212s))
- **Savit:** "an attorney general who would represent the people and not the powerful" ([Michigan Advance](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/eli-savit-wins-attorney-general-074412425.html))

### A mandate, and pressure to deliver
*Puri · Dingell · Brinks · Camilleri · McMorrow*

Leaders described the results as voters demanding change. Dingell warned that voters will send another message if Democrats don't deliver, and Brinks said progress comes from working across parties.

- **Puri:** "The people of Michigan spoke up tonight and said that they've had enough" ([Michigan Advance](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/return-trifecta-michigan-democrats-reclaim-100617155.html))
- **Dingell:** "if we don't deliver in the next few years you're going to see them send another loud message" ([post-election interview, NBC News 1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qt1jr4liIoc&t=110s))
- **Brinks:** "Real progress doesn't come from one office or one party alone." ([transition press conference, Detroit 2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xha9JCqPbBQ&t=150s))

### Pushing back on Trump and Washington
*Benson · McMorrow · El-Sayed*

Benson pledged to stand up to "any bully." McMorrow and El-Sayed criticized the president's record.

- **Benson:** "I will be unafraid to stand up to any bully, no matter how powerful" ([victory speech, Hudson's Detroit 10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X0E_y4UhEaM&t=612s))
- **McMorrow:** "He's done none of those things." ([interview with WWMT, election night 0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5FJOTQb2EU&t=49s))
- **El-Sayed:** "you've got a president who wants to defund the Department of Education. I think that's insane." ([post-election interview, WDIV Local 4 10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WL7ZL2UENhI&t=627s))

### Specific plans they named
*Benson · El-Sayed · Dingell*

Few concrete plans were named. Benson named a state voting rights act; El-Sayed named the federal PRO Act for unions, stopping the Medicaid cuts, and Medicare for All. Dingell named reversing the Medicaid cuts (see Part 2).

- **Benson:** "pass a voting rights act for the state of Michigan" ([victory speech, Hudson's Detroit 6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X0E_y4UhEaM&t=403s))
- **El-Sayed:** "I believe we need to pass the [PRO Act]." ([post-election interview, WDIV Local 4 3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WL7ZL2UENhI&t=223s))
- **Dingell:** "one of the first things that we are going to do is to reverse the cuts in Medicaid" ([post-election interview, FOX 2 Detroit 3:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBwr5WNZbwg&t=183s))

## Part 2: Health care focus

### Summary of what was said on health care

From election night (Nov. 3) through Nov. 4, 6 Democratic leaders appeared on camera in 9 speeches, press events and interviews (79 minutes of video). Three of them talked about health care: Abdul El-Sayed (21 statements), Debbie Dingell (5) and Jocelyn Benson (2). Winnie Brinks, Curtis Hertel Jr. and Mallory McMorrow did not mention health care.

El-Sayed said the first thing to do is stop the federal Medicaid cuts scheduled for January 2027. He said people who lose Medicaid will end up in emergency rooms, and that he expects some Republicans, especially from rural communities, to support reversing the cuts. He called for Medicare for All, said Blue Cross Blue Shield holds about 85% of Michigan's insurance market, and said insurers should not be able to raise rates by double digits or do stock buybacks.

Dingell said reversing the Medicaid cuts will be "one of the first things" the new U.S. House majority does, along with reinstating the ACA premium tax credit. She described constituents worried about losing Medicaid after receiving letters from the federal government. Benson listed health care with housing, child care and energy as costs her administration will make affordable, and pledged that women and girls will have access to the health care they need. Ranjeev Puri, quoted by the Michigan Advance, said he "watched healthcare be ripped away from our sick."

No leader mentioned hospitals, nurses or reimbursement, and no one named a specific state health bill. Attorney General-elect Eli Savit's victory remarks, as reported by the Michigan Advance and WLNS, did not mention health care.
