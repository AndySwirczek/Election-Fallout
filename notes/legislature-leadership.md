## 5. Lame duck (Nov.–Dec. 2026)
- **FY27 budget is done:** signed July 21, 2026, a $75.2B base (~$86.4B including ~$11B in Medicaid provider taxes and federal school funds). It includes $185M Medicaid stabilization through efficiency savings (the governor's proposed $780.4M in tobacco/gambling tax revenue was dropped) and $184.3M to implement federal H.R. 1 Medicaid/SNAP requirements. Whitmer also signed bipartisan health-cost bills on 7/22/2026 (contents not retrieved).
- **Pending/possible lame-duck items found:**
  - Hall's/Bollin's **HJR A**: a constitutional amendment requiring a two-thirds vote for lame-duck bills. It needs two-thirds of both chambers, so it is unlikely now.
  - The **$645M clawback dispute** (AG opinion Jan. 2026 found it unconstitutional; Hall vowed to sue; funds affected programs for kids with cancer, new moms, and infants). Resolution status not found.
  - Possible FY27 supplementals for federal H.R. 1 implementation (inference, not reported).
- **No reporting found** on a specific 2026 lame-duck health agenda: nurse staffing, PDAB, merger oversight, provider tax/HRA, or CON. Because Republicans hold the House floor through Dec. 31 and Hall has said lame duck should handle only "consensus issues," expect Democratic health priorities (PDAB, merger oversight) to wait for January 2027. This is analysis, not reporting.
- Senate session calendar: the Legislature site showed the Senate adjourned to Nov. 4, 2026. No published lame-duck session schedule was found.
